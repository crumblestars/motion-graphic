Changes for this advertising concept: excerpt selection, square and portrait cropping, resampling, variable-speed remapping, warm color grading, edited order, transitions and typography. The contributors do not endorse Blumenpyramide or this advertisement. Credits should accompany a redistributed or published version, for example in the publication description. The original project's music and the reference video's music are not reused.

The V3 soundtrack is an original synthesized electronic composition at 140 BPM, generated by `research/music-v3.py`.

The source footage was supplied as 1280 × 720 video. The delivered film is 720 × 1280; its editable composition is 1080 × 1920. Cropping and resampling cannot add source detail to the macro footage. There are no animated bouquet still images or decorative falling petals in V3.

Swishy, Pexels and Mixkit could not be inspected or downloaded from this environment because their hosts returned a network-proxy refusal (403). Their assets are not included and are not claimed as sources.
