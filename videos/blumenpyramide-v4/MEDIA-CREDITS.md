Changes for this advertising concept: excerpt selection, square and portrait cropping, resampling, variable-speed remapping, warm color grading, edited order, transitions and typography. The contributors do not endorse Blumenpyramide or this advertisement. Credits should accompany a redistributed or published version, for example in the publication description. The original project's music and the reference video's music are not reused.

The V4 soundtrack and search/click sounds are original synthesized audio at 119 BPM, generated by `research/music-v4.py`. No third-party soundtrack is included.

The source footage was supplied as 1280 × 720 video. The master and its editable composition are 1080 × 1920; the light delivery copy is 720 × 1280. Cropping and resampling cannot add source detail to the macro footage. There are no animated bouquet still images or decorative falling petals in V4.

Swishy, Pexels and Mixkit could not be inspected or downloaded from this environment because their hosts returned a network-proxy refusal (403). Their assets are not included and are not claimed as sources.

Logo: brand image supplied by the user in this conversation and prepared as a PNG asset through image preparation. It is used as the closing identity and in the search result card. The logo is not part of the stock-footage license. The new botanical vector linework is authored in the source composition.

GSAP 3.14.2 is used to author and sample the search-bar and result-card motion. Its package README and standard license reference are retained in `licenses/GSAP-README.md`: https://gsap.com/standard-license/ .
